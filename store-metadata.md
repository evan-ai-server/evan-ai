## Description (4000 chars max)
Evan AI is the smartest price scanner for resale shoppers and thrift hunters. Point your camera at any item and instantly know if it's a deal, how much it's worth, and where to sell it for more.

**How it works**
Scan any item with your camera. Evan AI's vision AI identifies the brand, model, and condition in seconds — then scans eBay, Amazon, and 10+ marketplaces to find the best price and alternatives up to 60% cheaper.

**Deal Intelligence**
• BEST DEAL / HIDDEN GEM / CHEAPER ALT badges on every result
• Market median price vs. what you're being charged
• Historical price charts — see if prices are rising or falling
• Buy Now vs. Wait recommendation backed by real sold data

**Flip Features (for resellers)**
• Flip Score — is this worth buying to resell?
• Flip Calculator — enter your costs, see your margin
• Price alerts — get notified the moment a watchlist item drops
• Negotiation Coach — AI-powered live haggling scripts
• Scan graveyard — items that sold way below asking price nearby

**Intel System**
• Ghost listing detector — is this seller legit?
• Condition vision — AI grades item condition from your photo
• Authenticity check — red flags for common fakes
• Dead stock leverage — use listing age to negotiate
• Rival reseller count — how many others are competing

**Your Profile**
• Scan history with total savings tracked
• Personalized flip identity (Sneaker Arbitrageur, Tech Flipper, etc.)
• Public savings profile — share what you've saved
• Achievement system with streaks and badges

**Pro (Evan AI Pro)**
Unlimited scans, price alerts for up to 50 items, and full intel access.
